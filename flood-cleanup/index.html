Floods start in many ways: heavy rain, storm surge, a river over its banks, a dead sump pump, a sewer backup or a major plumbing failure, and all of them call for fast professional help. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Day or night, 24/7, Brentwood Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Brentwood to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. With the standing water gone, our IICRC-certified technicians check the contamination level and apply the right sanitizing steps, including antimicrobial treatment, HEPA air filtration and disinfection of every affected surface. Materials beyond saving, like contaminated drywall, damaged insulation and saturated carpet pad, are removed and disposed of the right way.

Then the drying begins: high-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers of the LGR (low-grain refrigerant) type go in to pull moisture out of the building's structural parts. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Extend your downspouts to carry rain from the roof out past the foundation
  • In areas of the property that tend to flood, raise electrical systems and appliances off the floor
  • Store valuables and important papers off the basement floor in waterproof containers
  • Install a battery backup sump pump so you stay protected when storms knock out power
  • Look into a flood insurance policy, including when your home sits outside a mapped flood zone
  • Install backwater valves on sewer lines to stop sewage from backing up into your home

Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in Brentwood

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Stains along baseboards and lower walls that mark the flood level
  • A layer of silt, dirt or debris on the floor after water recedes
  • Storm damage that lets rain in through the roof or walls
  • Appliances such as a water heater, washer or dishwasher spilling water across the floor
  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation

Why Professional Flood Cleanup Matters

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.
